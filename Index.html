THE GROUP

BLACKPINK is a South Korean girl group formed by YG Entertainment, consisting of members Jisoo, Jennie, Rosé, and Lisa. The group debuted in August 2016 with their single album Square One, which featured "Whistle" and "Boombayah".

Known for their powerful performances, catchy music, and global influence, BLACKPINK has become one of the most successful K-pop groups worldwide, breaking numerous records and achieving unprecedented success.

HISTORY

2016 - DEBUT

BLACKPINK officially debuted on August 8, 2016, with the single album "Square One" containing tracks "Whistle" and "Boombayah". Both songs achieved massive success on Korean music charts.

2018 - INTERNATIONAL BREAKTHROUGH

Released "DDU-DU DDU-DU" which became the highest-charting song by a Korean girl group on the Billboard Hot 100. The group embarked on their first world tour.

2019 - COACHELLA

Made history as the first K-pop girl group to perform at Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival. Released "Kill This Love" which broke multiple YouTube records.

2020 - THE ALBUM

Released their first full-length album "THE ALBUM" featuring collaborations with Selena Gomez and Cardi B. The album debuted at number two on the Billboard 200.

2022 - BORN PINK ERA

Released their second studio album "BORN PINK" and embarked on the largest world tour by a K-pop girl group, performing in over 30 countries.
